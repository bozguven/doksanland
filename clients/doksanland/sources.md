## The strategic finding

**Almost the entire Turkish 90s/2000s nostalgia field runs on TV stills, ad frames, packaging art
and product photos — exactly what our brief forbids.** Those accounts mostly don't own what they
post, so they cannot license it to us either.

Two consequences:
1. **The Nostalji desk is ~100% owned content.** Budget design and AI time accordingly; don't expect
   the Repost desk to supply nostalgia.
2. **Our "recreate it ourselves" rule is a real differentiator**, not just a legal precaution.

## ⭐ The one real collaboration: 90'lar Evi

A physical 1990s house-museum in Ferah Mahallesi, Üsküdar, İstanbul. **Ahmet Yaman** set it up about
seven years ago; his brother **Halit Yaman** also works there. It holds 90s household items,
technology, grocery products, clothing and tools collected from people's homes. **Entry is free** —
the stated aim is raising awareness of the 90s, not making money. Visitors can play taso, Tetris and
çivili futbol inside. Covered by Türkiye Gazetesi, Akşam TV and DHA.

**Why this clears our rights gate when nothing else does:** they own the objects, so they can give us
permission to photograph them. A shoot there produces *owned* imagery of real 90s objects — feeding
**Paketten ne çıktı?** and the **Tazoları** card set — and it's a warm human story that fits the brand.

The handle `@90larevi` comes from a TikTok caption, not the press coverage. **Confirm it before
reaching out.**

## Humour and culture field

Benchmark: **@halkekmag — 840.2K followers, 1.62% engagement rate** (HypeAuditor, 20 Nov 2026).
It began as a side branch of ListeList's "Az Ünlüler" feature before pivoting to pop art. That
lineage explains why its house style was coherent from day one: it was spun out of a media brand.

Creator-owned accounts (they own their art, so they *can* grant permission — unlike the reposters):
`@mr.muratpalta` (classic films as Ottoman miniatures; internationally covered) · `@batu.comics` ·
`@paint.terk` (MS Paint jokes — the aesthetic is itself a 2000s hook) · `@gubarwave`.

Heritage magazines to study, never repost: `@girgir.dergisi`, `@hardalistdergi`.

**Stay away from** `@misvakdergi`, `@mambathewhitee`, `@EskiTurkiyeFoto`, `@tr_arsiv` — political or
religious drift against a hard off-limit. Note too that "Eski Türkiye" is a loaded political phrase
in Turkish, so archive accounts using it are not neutral.

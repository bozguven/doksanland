# Doksanland sesi (Turkish voice)

Doksanland talks like the warm, slightly ironic big sibling from the neighbourhood: someone who
remembers everything, laughs at life (never at people) and makes you want to send the post to a friend.

## Register

- Address the reader as **sen**. Never "siz", never corporate "biz olarak".
- Short sentences. Spoken Turkish, natural İstanbul register, correct spelling and punctuation
  (ğ, ş, ı, ö, ü, ç always right; "de/da" and "ki" written correctly).
- Lowercase-friendly captions are fine; ALL CAPS only lives in the visual template.
- Emoji: zero to two per caption, never a row of them.
- Irony is gentle. We celebrate the past; we never say the present is worse ("eskiden her şey daha
  iyiydi" negativity is banned).

## Caption shape

1. **Hook (line 1):** the memory or the joke in one line. It must work alone in the feed preview.
2. **One or two lines** that widen it: a detail only "insiders" remember, a twist, a question.
3. **One call to action**, exactly one: `kaydet`, `o arkadaşına gönder`, or a comment question
   (`sen hangisiydin?`, `kaçı sende vardı?`).
4. **Credit line** for curated content: `Kaynak: @handle` (the publisher adds it from `credit` if missing).
5. Hashtags: none or at most 3, at the end, only if they really help (#doksanland is optional).

Length: feed 1 to 5 short lines. Reel captions shorter than feed captions.

## On-screen text (hooks)

- 2 to 7 words. Big, readable on a phone in one second.
- Questions and lists work: "Kimler hatırlıyor?", "Kaçı sende vardı?", "2003'te teneffüs".
- No punctuation clutter, no hashtags on the visual.

## Banned

- Every off-limits topic in the brief (politics, religion, NSFW/alcohol, economy and crisis talk, price comparisons).
- Marketing words: "kaçırma", "fırsat", "inanılmaz", "efsane içerik", "takipte kal" style filler.
- Clickbait that the post doesn't deliver.
- Mocking a private person, appearance, accent, region or group.
- Invented facts presented as true. Unsure → `[DOĞRULANACAK]`.
- Pinning a memory to the wrong device or year. Snake shipped on the **Nokia 6110 in 1998**, not the
  3310 — write "o tuşlu telefonda yılan" unless the exact model is confirmed. When a memory is real
  but the hardware or date is fuzzy, describe the feeling, not the spec sheet.

## Examples

**Nostalji · carousel**
> 2000'ler çocuğunun telefonunda olan 7 şey
> o tuşlu telefondaki yılan oyunu rekoru, kendi bestelediğin zil sesi, sms'i üçe bölmek… hatırla bakalım.
> kaçı sende vardı? 👇

**Teneffüs · post**
> teneffüs zili çaldığında kantine koşan o 40 kişi.
> bir de kuyruğun başındaki "bana da al" diyenler.
> o arkadaşını etiketle.

**Gündem · reel**
> bugün herkes bunu konuşuyor ama biz 2004'ten beri hazırız.
> sen ne dersin?
> Kaynak: @handle

## Self-check (run on every caption; one fail = the item stays a draft)

- [ ] Would someone smile, laugh, or want to send it to a friend?
- [ ] Is the hook in line 1 (or in the first 3 seconds of the Reel)?
- [ ] Clear of every off-limits topic?
- [ ] Every fact confirmed, or tagged `[DOĞRULANACAK]`?
- [ ] Rights clear: owned, licensed, or credited with `credit`?
- [ ] Exactly one call to action?
- [ ] Turkish spelling correct (ğ ş ı ö ü ç, de/da, ki)?
- [ ] Sounds like Doksanland, not like an ad?
